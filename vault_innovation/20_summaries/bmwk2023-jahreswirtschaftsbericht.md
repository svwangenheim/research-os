## Detailed summary

The 2023 Annual Economic Report of the Federal Government sets out economic and fiscal policy priorities under the framework of a "Sozial-ökologische Marktwirtschaft." The report's overarching framing is that Germany faces a dual challenge: managing the immediate energy-price and inflation crisis while accelerating the structural transformation needed for long-run competitiveness and climate neutrality by 2045.

**Structure:**

- *Section A–B (pp. 9–26):* Macroeconomic stabilization and current conditions
- *Section C (pp. 27–43):* Wohlstand erneuern — Energieversorgung sichern, Transformation beschleunigen (includes Zukunftspaket, Figure 4, p. 30)
- *Section D (pp. 44–58):* Angebotspolitische Agenda für mehr Fachkräfte, Investitionen und Innovation — primary innovation policy section; covers skilled labour, corporate transformation support, and competition policy
- *Section E (pp. 59–71):* Strategische Souveränität stärken — technological sovereignty, Schlüsseltechnologien, trade policy
- *Sections F–H (pp. 72–106):* Social policy, public investment, climate
- *Special Chapter (pp. 107–133):* Welfare measurement and societal progress

**Key innovation policy framing:**

The Geleitwort identifies three investment priorities: (1) Klimaschutz und Schlüsseltechnologien, (2) technologische Kompetenzen, (3) Energieforschung. The government commits to "öffentliche Investitionen weiter steigern und auf Zukunftsprojekte fokussieren" and mobilizing private capital through tax incentives (Forschungszulage implied) and instruments like Klimaschutzverträge.

The supply-side agenda (Angebotspolitik) is positioned as a response to the energy price shock — investing in technology and transformation rather than only demand stabilization. This is the policy context in which the 2023 Förderkatalog allocations were made.

## Research question

N/A (official government report). Relevant policy question: how should Germany align R&D support, industrial transformation, and labour market policy to manage the 2022–23 crisis while accelerating long-run structural transformation?

## Core argument or contribution

Germany must pursue "kluge Angebotspolitik" combining crisis stabilization with structural transformation. Schlüsseltechnologien, Energieforschung, and climate-tech investment are the priority areas for public R&D. This is the government's official framing of the purpose of the Bundesinnovationshaushalt in 2023.

## Key findings (policy-relevant for this project)

1. **Schlüsseltechnologien as explicit priority:** Framed as central to both technological sovereignty and climate transition — relevant context for the HTAD sector classification in the Förderkatalog analysis.
2. **Supply-side focus on Zukunftsinvestitionen:** The 2023 agenda explicitly orients R&D and innovation support toward transformation-enabling investments; the intended TRL profile of 2023 grants was supposedly more forward-looking/lower TRL than in prior years.
3. **Zukunftspaket:** Figure 4 (p. 30) maps government investment programs against the transformation agenda; useful reference for budget context.
4. **Klimaschutzverträge as new instrument:** Carbon Contracts for Difference as an innovative deep-decarbonization instrument — relevant for understanding where near-market (high-TRL) industrial transformation investments sit in the government's portfolio.
5. **Transformation context:** The energy crisis is framed as both constraint and opportunity for innovation-driven transformation — the political economy context in which 2023 Förderkatalog allocations were decided.

## Limitations

- Official government document — strategic/political framing, not independent analysis
- Does not provide quantitative R&D allocation data at programme or TRL level
- Pre-dates the post-Ampel political reconfiguration; some priorities shifted by 2024–25
- German-language only

## Implications for the Bundesinnovationshaushalt project

1. **Policy context for 2023 allocations:** Establishes the official government framing under which 2023 Förderkatalog grants were made. Stated priorities (Schlüsseltechnologien, Energieforschung, Transformation) can be compared against the actual TRL and sector distribution in the FK data.
2. **Schlüsseltechnologien alignment test:** If the FK analysis shows that a large share of funding goes to non-Schlüsseltechnologien sectors at high TRL, this creates a documented gap between stated priorities (this report) and actual allocation.
3. **Zukunftspaket context:** Figure 4 provides a budget map of transformation investments — useful background for contextualizing the Förderkatalog within the broader innovation budget.
