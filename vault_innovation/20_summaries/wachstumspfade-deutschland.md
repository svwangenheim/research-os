## Key findings

**Six Schlüsseltechnologien:** 1. **Künstliche Intelligenz (KI):** 4-level hierarchy (Applications → AI Models: LLM/LMM/Weltmodelle → AI Platforms → Compute Infrastructure); 5 HTAD focus technologies 2. **Quantentechnologien:** Computing, Sensing, Communication/Cryptography, Simulation; German strengths in sensing, weak in computing 3. **Mikroelektronik:** Chip design, advanced packaging, equipment & materials, specialty applications; Germany competitive in equipment, weak in leading-edge logic 4. **Biotechnologie:** Genomics/synthetic biology, biomanufacturing, computational biology, cell/gene therapy; German potential in biomanufacturing 5. **Fusion/Neue Energiesysteme:** Fusion reactors, advanced fission, grid-scale storage, hydrogen infrastructure 6. **Klimaneutrale Mobilität:** EV/fuel cell drivetrains, autonomous vehicles, sustainable aviation fuels, charging infrastructure - Cross-cutting finding: Germany competitive on component/subsystem level, weak on system integration and platform plays - Radical innovation deficit: most German firms excel at TRL 6-9 but underinvest in TRL 1-4 in these fields
