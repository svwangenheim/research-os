## Key findings

- 63% of projects start at TRL3 or lower
- By project end: 47% reach TRL6–TRL8; only 12% remain at TRL1–TRL3
- Most projects advance **2–3 TRL steps** per grant period (~36–48 months)
- Most common trajectories: TRL3→TRL5 (189 projects), TRL2→TRL4 (179 projects), TRL6→TRL9 (178 projects)
- 3% of projects report zero TRL progression; <2% advance 6+ steps (usually non-engineering projects with loose TRL interpretation)
- **68% of all funding** is concentrated in projects ending at TRL5–TRL7
- TRL8–TRL9 projects: only 16% of funding
- At TRL1–TRL2: universities (HES) receive ~60% of funding
- At TRL8: companies (PRC) receive ~67% of funding; at TRL9, ~60%
- Research organisations (REC) funded across all TRL levels → bridge role between academia and industry | Pillar | Focus TRL range | Key patterns | |---|---|---| | Pillar I (Excellent Science) | TRL1–TRL4 | 51% start at TRL1; ERC concentrated at TRL3 by end; MSCA distributed broadly; INFRA clusters at TRL6+ | | Pillar II (Global Challenges) | TRL3–TRL7 | 76% end at TRL5–TRL7; dominated by Clusters 4 (Digital/Industry/Space) and 5 (Climate/Energy) |
