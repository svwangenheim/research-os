## Key findings

Total business sector innovation expenditure: €203.4bn (2023), +6.6% vs 2022 - Current expenditure: €155.0bn - Investment: €48.4bn - Breakdown by innovation type and industry available in full report - Provides the macro context for Germany's private R&D landscape that complements the public Förderkatalog data - MIP tracks: product innovations, process innovations, organizational innovations, marketing innovations

## Implications for LLM research or the project domain

Primary data reference for Germany's overall private innovation landscape. Provides the denominator context for federal direct subsidies (FK projects represent a subset of total public R&D support). The MIP's firm-level panel structure (via MIP/CIS) is the underlying dataset for multiple ZEW subsidy evaluation papers cited in this project (Czarnitzki, Aschhoff, Cherif).
