# Hintergrundpapier — DZ House Style

> **STUB — needs DZ house-style calibration (see dz-core / brand-voice skill).**
> No dedicated writer-dz template exists yet for this format — the structure below is a
> reasonable interpolation between the Fachtext (rigor) and the Geldbrief (accessibility).
> Calibrate against real published Hintergrundpapiere before treating any rule as authoritative.

The Hintergrundpapier is a medium-length (~2500–4000 words) DZ background paper in German. It explains the context, mechanisms, and stakes of an issue for a reader who needs to understand the terrain before forming a position. It is more explanatory than the Geldbrief and less method-heavy than the Fachtext, but it still takes a DZ position.

## §1 Purpose and Register

- **Audience:** decision-makers and advisors who need orientation on a complex issue, not a full empirical study.
- **Register:** explanatory and structured, but opinionated where the evidence supports a position. Institutionelles Wir throughout.
- Explain mechanisms plainly; keep technical terms exact but define them on first use.

## §2 Structure (~2500–4000 words)

```
# Titel — issue + angle
**[Autor:in]** | [DD.MM.YYYY] | Kontakt: [email@dezernatzukunft.org]

> Kurzfassung (150–250 words): worum es geht → warum jetzt → die zentrale Einsicht → DZ-Einordnung

## 1. Ausgangslage        concrete context + numbers; why the issue matters now (Kontext-Eröffnung)
## 2. Wie es funktioniert   the mechanism / institutional setup explained (Kausal-Absatz-led)
## 3. Die Streitpunkte      competing positions, steelmanned, then the DZ reading (Kontrast-Absatz-led)
## 4. Einordnung und offene Fragen   what follows, what remains uncertain, optional recommendations
```

**Kurzfassung:** an orientation, not a summary of results. Ends with the DZ Einordnung (how to read the issue), not necessarily a hard recommendation.

## §3 Argument Moves

Same move vocabulary as the Fachtext, weighted toward **Kontext-Eröffnung** and **Kausal-Absatz** (explaining) and **Kontrast-Absatz** (adjudicating debates). Use the **Daten-Move** to anchor the stakes with a striking statistic. Recommendations, when present, use the **Empfehlungs-Absatz** but may be framed as options with trade-offs rather than single imperatives.

## §4 Language

- Active voice, concrete subjects.
- Numbers with units and context; explain what each number implies for the reader.
- One claim per sentence; vary sentence length.
- Sourced claims: inline hyperlinks (Geldbrief convention) or Autor-Jahr (Fachtext convention) — pick one per document and stay consistent.

### §4.1 Forbidden words and phrases

> Calibrate against the real DZ style guide. Inherit the Geldbrief/Fachtext forbidden lists:
> significance inflation, promotional language, throat-clearing openers, and evasive closers
> („bleibt abzuwarten").

## §5 Section-Heading Patterns

Descriptive and oriented to the reader's questions ("Wie es funktioniert", "Die Streitpunkte"), stated as content rather than bare structural labels. Avoid „Hintergrund" / „Fazit" as standalone headings.

## §6 Tone

Orienting first, opinionated second. Lay out the terrain honestly, steelman the disagreements, then give the DZ reading. Where the evidence is genuinely open, say so specifically rather than hedging vaguely.
