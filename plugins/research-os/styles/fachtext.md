The Fachtext is a long (~5000–8000 words), rigorous DZ analytical paper in German (occasionally English). It carries a full argument with methods, evidence, and numbered policy recommendations. Same argument discipline as the academic writer; Markdown output, not LaTeX.

## §1 Purpose and Register

- **Audience:** specialists and policy professionals who want the full reasoning.
- **Register:** precise, opinionated, evidence-dense. Institutionelles Wir throughout.
- Technical economic terms are kept exact (Potenzialwachstum, Konnexitätsprinzip) — do not simplify away precision.

## §2 Structure (~5000–8000 words)

```
# Titel — descriptive, not generic
**[Vorname Nachname]** | [DD.MM.YYYY] | Kontakt: [email@dezernatzukunft.org]
`#HASHTAG1 #HASHTAG2 #HASHTAG3`

## Zusammenfassung / Executive Summary   (self-contained: bullet findings with numbers + bullet imperatives)
## 1. Einleitung        [800–1200] Kontext → Forschungsfrage → Kernergebnis → Papierstruktur
## 2. Methodik/Daten/Konzept  [800–1500] Methodik-Absatz → Daten-Move → Kausal-Absatz → Einschränkungs-Absatz
## 3. Ergebnisse        [800–1500] Befund-Absatz → Kausal-Absatz → Kontrast-Absatz → Daten-Move
## 4. (weiterer Ergebnisabschnitt, falls nötig)
## 5. Schluss und Politikempfehlungen  [500–800] Zusammenfassung → Einschränkungen → nummerierte Empfehlungen (2–5)
## Anhang / Appendix (optional)
## Literatur / References  (alphabetical, Harvard-style, DOIs where available)
```

**Executive Summary:** self-contained — functions for someone who reads nothing else. Bullet findings with numbers; bullet recommendations as imperatives. No section-number references, no methodology beyond one sentence.

## §3 Argument Moves

Same move types as the Geldbrief (Befund-, Kontext-, Methodik-, Kontrast-, Kausal-, Einschränkungs-, Empfehlungs-Absatz). The Einschränkungs-Absatz belongs in the Schluss, stated honestly and specifically — not vague boilerplate, not hidden.

- Every section opener states the finding or a Kontext-Eröffnung — never method setup, never history.
- Kausalitätsketten explicit: say how A leads to B with a causal connector (dadurch, daraus folgt, weil).
- Hedge once per claim, then commit and argue forward. No multi-layer hedging.

## §4 Language

- Active voice, concrete subjects in result sentences.
- Numbers with units and context always; numbers in the body match the Executive Summary exactly.
- Citations: **Harvard (Autor-Jahr) in parentheses** — not inline hyperlinks.

### §4.1 Forbidden words and phrases

> Calibrate against the real DZ style guide. Starting set:
> significance inflation („bahnbrechend", „wegweisend"), promotional language („revolutionär",
> „transformativ" without quantified evidence), throat-clearing openers („Es ist wichtig zu
> betonen, dass", „An dieser Stelle sei darauf hingewiesen"), and „Abschließend lässt sich sagen".

## §5 Section-Heading Patterns

Numbered, descriptive finding statements — not structural labels. Not „2. Methodik" but „2. Fiskalische Kosten-Arbeitsplatz-Relation: Konzept und Berechnung". Executive Summary, Appendix, and References are exempt from numbering.

## §6 Tone

Rigorous and committed. Opinioniert, belegbar: take positions, back every normative claim with evidence. Recommendations are concrete imperatives, never conditional or descriptive.
